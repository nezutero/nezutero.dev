*Last updated: 20 October 2026*

The following statement was originally written by [Anna Liberty](https://gracefulliberty.com/llms/).
It resonates deeply with who I am and who I want to become. I am grateful to be able to use it as a
basis and make a few minor changes to it so that I can use it to express my values.

---

I do not use LLMs or other “generative AI” systems to write code or perform other day-to-day tasks, nor do I accept such materials from others as contributions to open-source code.

I do not discuss my life or code with LLMs.

I do not consult LLMs for projects, contributions, assignments, research, or anything else.

As far as possible and practicable, I have disabled LLM-integration in my tools or replaced tools that don't let me do so.

Requests for me to use LLMs or read content generated by LLMs will be met with disappointment and gentle refusal.

I want to use, develop, improve my skills and engage with other people in the process.

---

For programming, I write code myself, or generate it deterministically from higher-level instructions.

For writing, I use my brain and write it myself, revising it as needed.

For searching, I use domain-specific search tools and specific websites.

For learning, as far as possible and practicable, I seek out books, tutorials, and other educational material made by people.

For extra support, I ask experts in forums, chatrooms, or via email.
